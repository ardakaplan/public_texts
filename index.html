Privacy Policy for RDA Note

Last updated: August 20, 2026

RDA Note respects your privacy and is designed to collect as little user data as possible.

Notes and User Content

All notes and text entered into RDA Note are stored locally on the user's device.

RDA Note does not upload, transmit, or store the content of your notes on external servers.

Crash Reporting

RDA Note uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes and improve the stability and reliability of the application.

When the application crashes or encounters an error, Firebase Crashlytics may automatically collect technical information such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Application version
  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Diagnostic and application performance information
  • Technical identifiers related to the application installation or device

This information is used only for diagnosing technical problems, fixing crashes, and improving the application.

The content of notes entered by the user is not intentionally included in crash reports.

Firebase Crashlytics is provided by Google. Data processed through Firebase is subject to Google's applicable privacy and data processing terms.

Accounts and Advertising

RDA Note does not require users to create an account or sign in.

RDA Note does not contain advertising and does not sell user information.

Data Sharing

RDA Note does not sell personal information to third parties. Technical diagnostic information may be processed by Google through Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and application stability purposes.

Data Storage

Notes created in RDA Note are stored locally on the user's device. Deleting the application's data or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored notes.

Children's Privacy

RDA Note does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own features. Technical diagnostic information may still be processed automatically by Firebase Crashlytics when the application is used.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
