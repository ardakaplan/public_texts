Last updated: August 31, 2026

All About Us Lite does not collect personal information or user data.

All About Us Lite does not send your personal data to any external server.

All About Us Lite does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose your personal information to third parties because the application does not collect such information.

Your application data is stored locally on your device.

Because we do not receive or store your data on our servers, we cannot access, recover, or remotely delete your locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

All About Us Lite does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Since the application does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer, no personal information is knowingly stored by us.

7. Security

Because your All About Us Lite content remains locally on your device, the security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.
